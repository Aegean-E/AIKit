## 1. Scope and Definitions

1.1 **Covered Research Material** means the source code, object code, documentation, notebooks,
scripts, model code and project-provided datasets that a project identifies as offered under
ASL-1.1. A project does this by carrying this text as its `LICENSE` and naming ASL-1.1 in its
README release notes.

1.2 **Project Data** means datasets, annotations, model weights and other research artefacts that
the project ships or links as part of the Covered Research Material, but only to the extent the
project owns them or may sublicense them. Third-party datasets keep their own licenses and
notices. Project Data must not include personal data unless the project states a lawful basis and
a dataset-specific notice.

1.3 **Third-Party Components** (libraries, models, datasets, tools and platform services) keep
their own licenses. ASL-1.1 grants only what Licensor owns or may sublicense.

1.4 **Non-Commercial** means not primarily intended for commercial advantage or monetary
compensation, and not Commercial or Organizational Use under AL-1.1 Section 4.1, except for the
non-commercial research and teaching uses Section 2 expressly grants to universities, public
research bodies and non-profit funders.

1.5 Every other capitalized term has the meaning AL-1.1 gives it. AL-1.1 Sections 6 (Patent
Defense), 7 (Ownership and Contributions) and 8 to 15 apply to ASL-1.1 as if written here, except
where Sections 2 to 6 below add science terms; a conflict is resolved in favour of this document.
