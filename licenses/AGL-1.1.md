## 3. The DRM-Free Promise

1. **Licensor distributes the Covered Game DRM-free.** A build Licensor releases under AGL-1.1
   requires no activation server, online check, account binding, install limit, mandatory client,
   launcher or client-side license enforcement to install or play. A storefront may require an
   account to download the build; once downloaded, the build does not.
2. **Do not add DRM.** If You distribute a mod, patch or code change permitted by Section 2.5, You
   must not add digital rights management, activation, always-online requirements or play-blocking
   telemetry. This is a condition of the license, not a request.
3. **No anti-consumer technical measures.** You must not use anti-cheat, kernel drivers or account
   bans to prevent the personal play, modding, backup, recording or streaming Section 2 allows.
   Protecting a legitimate multiplayer service from cheating is permitted; using anti-cheat to
   disable single-player rights is not.
4. **No circumvention.** AL-1.1 Section 5.7 (no circumvention of technical measures) still applies.
   Under AGL-1.1 there should be no such measure in a Licensor build; if one appears by mistake,
   report it to `aegean-licensing@proton.me` instead of relying on this section. Good-faith
   security research on a copy You are licensed to use, and coordinated disclosure, are permitted;
   this section does not forbid testing Your own copy or reporting a vulnerability.
5. **Online services may end.** Licensor may change or discontinue online features, servers or
   accounts. The offline build You already have remains playable to the extent it does not depend
   on those services, and Licensor will not intentionally break offline play.
6. **Consumer rights.** Nothing in this License limits mandatory consumer rights in Your country,

7. **No compatibility warranty.** A DRM-free build may be copied, but Licensor does not promise
